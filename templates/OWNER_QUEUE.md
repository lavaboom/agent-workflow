# Waiting on the owner

Every open decision and owner action, in one list, the ones holding up the most work first. Agents add to
it; the owner answers here or in chat. How it works: `C:/code/agent-workflow/AGENT_PROTOCOL.md` → "The owner
queue".

Each item: what is asked, what it blocks, by when, the options and a recommendation, and the answer. A
task waiting on one says `Blocked on: Q-NNN` in its `status.md`. Answered items move to
[Answered](#answered) with the date; their answer also goes into the spec or doc that needed it.
